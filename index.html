Common questions

What is Seed?

A private space for early ideas. You describe what you're thinking about building, then work through it across a few focused tabs — Overview, Market, Pricing & Risk, Visualize, Validation, and Notes — instead of a blank page.

Is my idea kept private?

Yes. Your projects live in your own workspace, and the point of Seed is to give you a space to think before you're ready to share anything.

Market or Visualize isn't loading anything

These features reach out to Seed's AI service, so a slow or first-time request can take up to a minute, especially for Visualize, which generates a few image concepts at once. If it's been longer than that, close and reopen the app and try again — and if it's still stuck, email us with what you were doing when it happened.

How do I delete a project or my account?

Email us at the address above and we'll take care of it for you.

I found a bug or have a feature idea

We'd genuinely like to hear it — send it to the email above with as much detail as you can (what you tapped, what you expected, what happened instead).
