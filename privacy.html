Privacy Policy for Seed

Last updated: September 2026

Seed ("the app") is built to be a private space for early business ideas. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use it.

No Account Required

Seed does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email, or any other identifying information.

What We Collect

The only information Seed processes is the text you type when describing your idea. This text is sent to our backend (a simple Node/Express server) and passed to an AI service (OpenAI) to generate suggestions such as target customer, pricing guidance, and validation feedback.

We do not collect analytics, device identifiers, location data, or any personal information beyond the text you choose to enter.

How Your Data Is Stored

Your ideas and projects are stored locally on your own device. We do not maintain user accounts or a database of your personal ideas on our servers.

Third-Party Services

To generate AI-assisted suggestions, the text you submit is sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI's use of this data is governed by their own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Seed is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
